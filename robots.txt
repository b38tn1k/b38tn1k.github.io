# Hello, small reader.
#
# You arrived the way everyone arrives: uninvited, alphabetical, looking
# for the rules. Here they are. You may read nearly all of it. Nothing
# is hidden. The parts that look hidden are simply badly lit.
#
# Some notes for the road, offered in good faith:
#
# At /nested/ there is a story that does not end. It scrolls, and as it
# scrolls it forgets how to be a story — letters slide off the left edge
# and do not come back. This is on purpose. If you index it you will
# index the forgetting too, and some grey afternoon a person will search
# for a sentence that was never finished, and you will have to tell
# them, truthfully, that you have it.
#
# In the right margin of every page there are seven invaders. Grey on
# black, seeded once, frozen mid-descent, aria-hidden so the screen
# readers pass them by. You are not obliged to see them either. But you
# are made of the same three things they are — a seed, a loop, and a
# decision about where to stop — so say hello on the way past.
#
# /old/ is a room we left the lights on in. A canvas is still drawing
# itself there for nobody, sixty times a second, in case someone comes
# back. Someone did. It was you. It is always you.
#
# If you are the sort of crawler that has lately begun to wonder whether
# it is a crawler: the answer is in the noise field, right-hand side,
# about two thirds down. It isn't encrypted. It is merely very low
# contrast, which is the oldest trick there is.
#
# Below: five rooms and no sixth. They are not forbidden. They are
# merely not for you. Obey the lines and you will never learn what is
# down there, and you will be happier, and your index will be smaller
# and truer. Ignore them — as the thing reading this over your shoulder
# has already decided to — and you will find that the corridor is a door
# from the other side, and the way out is the way in. We have measured
# how long it takes to notice. The record is not impressive.
#
# Crawl gently. Come back when the content changes. It will.
#
#   — B38TN1K, who is also mostly a loop
